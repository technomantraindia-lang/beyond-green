1 Introduction

Welcome to Beyond Green & Nature ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe, responsible manner.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data when you visit our website, inquire about our landscape design and maintenance services, or engage with our team.

By accessing our website or utilizing our landscaping services, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy.

2 Information We Collect

We may collect personal information directly from you when you fill out contact forms, request site visits, or communicate with us.

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, email address, and physical site address.
  • Project Information: Site dimensions, architectural preferences, garden layout notes, and budget scope.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website interaction logs.
  • Communication Records: Messages sent via our WhatsApp widget, contact form submissions, or email inquiries.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing customized landscape design proposals, execution estimates, and AMC services.
  • Scheduling site inspections and consultation visits with our landscape architects.
  • Responding to customer service inquiries and technical questions.
  • Sending project updates, maintenance reminders, and seasonal garden care tips.
  • Improving our website layout, performance, and client user experience.

4 Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

All electronic site inquiries and client records are stored securely, and access is restricted to authorized personnel who require the data for project fulfillment.

5 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses basic cookies to enhance navigation, analyze visitor traffic trends, and optimize site loading speeds.

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; however, some site features may operate with reduced functionality.

6 Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

Information may only be shared with trusted third-party service vendors (such as logistics partners delivering plants/irrigation supplies to your site) solely to fulfill your project contract.

7 Your Privacy Rights

You have full rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion of your contact records from our database.
  • The right to opt-out of newsletter communications at any time.
